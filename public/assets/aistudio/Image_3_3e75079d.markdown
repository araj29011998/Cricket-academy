## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an elite, authoritative, and aspirational digital atmosphere for high-performance cricket training while remaining structured, dependable, and approachable for parents and young athletes. 

The aesthetic fuses **Athletic Precision** with **Modern Sport Prestige**. It avoids juvenile tropes in favor of sharp, performance-driven visual mechanics: punchy condensed display typography reminiscent of stadium scoreboards and international test series graphics, clean structural panels, tactical turf-tinted surfaces, and high-impact accent moments. The emotional response is focused, professional, and inspiring—signaling rigorous coaching standards, Olympic-level discipline, and premier athletic mentorship.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the heritage and kinetic energy of elite cricket:

- **Dominant Base & Primary Surfaces:** Deep Pitch Green (`#0B3B24`) and Deep Turf Onyx (`#061F13`) build an authoritative, immersive stadium feel. These rich botanical dark tones replace generic pure blacks to establish prestige.
- **Accents & Conversions:** Cricket Leather Red (`#D92D20`) signals urgent actions, high-energy calls-to-action (trial bookings, match registrations), and performance telemetry. Championship Gold (`#F59E0B`) honors excellence, elite player tiering, trophies, and premium masterclass badges.
- **Support & Contrast:** Crisp Chalk White (`#FFFFFF`) provides stark field-line readability for key figures, metrics, and high-impact headlines. Slate Charcoal (`#1E293B`) offers neutral containment for data cards, borders, and sub-panels.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes tension between the condensed, commanding punch of **Oswald** and the neutral utility of **Inter**, bound together by the geometric precision of **Montserrat**:

- **Headlines & Metrics (Oswald):** Rendered uppercase or title-case to evoke stadium scoreboards and broadcast title cards. Numbers in Oswald carry athletic density, ideal for bowling speeds (km/h), batting averages, and program counts.
- **Body & Explanatory Copy (Inter):** High legibility for schedules, curriculum outlines, safety guidelines, and parent communications. Ensures readability across dense information modules.
- **Labels, Badges, & Navigation (Montserrat):** Set in bold, tracking-spaced uppercase to structure navigation tiers, category badges, and athletic tags cleanly.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display multi-axis content (coach bios, training schedules, performance stats, cohort tiers) cleanly:

- **Desktop (12 Columns, `margin-lg: 4rem`, `gutter-lg: 2rem`):** Broad modular panels allow simultaneous presentation of video analysis demos, cohort tables, and stat counters. Max content container caps at `1280px` for high-readability alignment.
- **Tablet (8 Columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`):** Cards snap to 2-column or 4-column groupings. Metric modules shift into side-by-side comparative grids.
- **Mobile (4 Columns, `margin-sm: 1rem`, `gutter-sm: 1rem`):** Single-column vertical stacks. Horizontal scroll carousels house time-sensitive program tiers and match schedules to avoid excessive page height.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp tonal zoning combined with stadium-inspired turf depth rather than soft, diffuse SaaS dropshadows:

- **Base Canvas:** Deep Turf Onyx (`#061F13`) provides the infinite foundation.
- **Layer 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pitch Green (`#0B3B24`) with an ultra-fine 1px border of Slate Charcoal at 40% opacity (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.4)`).
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Modals):** Tinted Slate Charcoal surfaces (`#1E293B`) paired with high-contrast outlines (`1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)` on hover).
- **Shadow Profile:** Crisp, directional athletic shadows using deep dark green cast: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(6, 31, 19, 0.6)`. Hover states introduce subtle rim-light highlights using Championship Gold (`#F59E0B`) or Chalk White glows.

## Shapes

The design language uses soft-cornered, engineered profiles (Level 1: 0.25rem base radius). 

Curved geometries are kept intentionally low to maintain an athletic, technical, and disciplined silhouette. Buttons, metric enclosures, and training program modules feature precise 4px corners, reinforcing clinical execution. Circular pill shapes are reserved exclusively for compact status tags (e.g., "U-17 Squad", "Slots Available").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book Trial / Register):** Cricket Leather Red (`#D92D20`) background, Crisp Chalk White text in Montserrat Bold (`label-lg`), uppercase with 0.08em letter-spacing. 4px border radius. Hover introduces a dynamic 2px forward elevation with deep red ambient glow (`rgba(217, 45, 32, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Action (View Curriculum / Faculty):** Transparent surface with 1.5px solid Chalk White or Championship Gold outline, text matching border tone. On hover, fills with a 15% tinted color wash.
- **Tertiary / Text Buttons:** Inter SemiBold, underlined with 2px offset, defaulting to Championship Gold (`#F59E0B`).

### Athletic Badges & Chips
- **Tier & Cohort Badges:** Compact labels using 2px radius or pill radius, set against Deep Turf Onyx with a 1px border.
- **Accreditation & Awards:** Championship Gold text on a dark translucent background (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`), paired with a gold micro-icon (stars, wickets, ribbons).

### Cards & Stat Panels
- **Program & Coach Cards:** Background in Deep Pitch Green (`#0B3B24`) with sharp 4px corners. Subdued turf texture or angled seam line motifs in the header. Clear visual separation between coach credentials, age brackets, and fee schedules.
- **Metric Callouts:** High-contrast scoreboard-style data blocks. Large Oswald numbers (`metric-stat`) in Crisp Chalk White or Championship Gold, flanked by small Montserrat uppercase labels denoting units (e.g., "142 KM/H BOWLING PEAK", "98% MATCH RETENTION").

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Inputs:** Dark Slate Charcoal surface (`#1E293B`), 1px muted border, Crisp Chalk White user text with placeholder in faded slate. Focus state triggers a clean 1.5px Championship Gold border and no rounded expansion.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Crisp square (checkbox, 2px radius) and circle (radio) selectors with 1.5px Chalk White borders. Checked states fill with Cricket Leather Red (`#D92D20`) displaying a white sharp vector checkmark.

### Additional Product Components
- **Batting/Bowling Telemetry Tracker:** Visual lane graphic depicting pitch bounce zones and wagon wheels using Championship Gold and Leather Red telemetry overlays.
- **Academy Schedule Ribbon:** Horizontal timeline with real-time session indicators ("Net Session Active", "Rain Delay Protocol", "High Performance Camp").
